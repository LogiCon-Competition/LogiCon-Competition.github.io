Proposed NeurIPS 2026 Competition

LogiCon Competition

Benchmarking LLM-based Contradiction Detection

Contradiction Detection evaluation of LLMs on their ability do detect real-world inter-context conflicts of different semantic/logical bases and explicitness levels. This website is the public hub for the proposal and, if accepted, the competition launch.

Participant flow

  1. Register on Codabench.
  2. Download data (and optionally support code) from this website.
  3. Run local validation with the starter kit.
  4. Upload predictions to the Codabench submission phase.
  5. Codabench validates, scores, logs, and updates the leaderboard.

Terms and Conditions

By registering for the CoSEC Depth Challenge, downloading the dataset, or submitting predictions to the evaluation server, participants agree to the following terms.

1. Academic and Research Use

The CoSEC dataset, including event data, frame data, depth annotations, calibration files, metadata, and benchmark tools, is provided for academic research and challenge participation purposes only. Commercial use, redistribution, sublicensing, or monetization of the dataset is prohibited without prior written permission from the organizers.

2. Data Usage

Participants may use the official CoSEC training data for model development. External datasets, pretrained models, synthetic data, and additional training resources are allowed only if they are publicly available or legally accessible, and must be disclosed upon request. The test set must only be used for official evaluation. Participants must not attempt to access, reconstruct, manually annotate, reverse-engineer, or infer the hidden ground-truth depth data.

3. Submission Rules

Each submission must follow the official submission format, including filenames, data format, and folder structure. Invalid, corrupted, incomplete, or incorrectly formatted submissions may be rejected by the organizers.

4. Fair Use and Anti-Cheating Policy

Participants must not probe or exploit the evaluation server, manually tune predictions on the test set, use hidden test-set ground truth, submit hard-coded predictions, or use multiple accounts or teams to gain an unfair advantage. Any form of cheating, data leakage, or violation of the challenge rules may result in disqualification and removal from the leaderboard.

5. Technical Report and Verification

Top-ranked teams may be required to submit a short technical report describing their method, training data, model architecture, loss functions, post-processing steps, and external resources. The organizers may request code, logs, model outputs, or additional information for result verification.

6. Liability and Disclaimer

The dataset, benchmark tools, and evaluation server are provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. The organizers are not liable for any damages arising from the use of the dataset or participation in this challenge. The organizers reserve the right to modify the challenge rules, evaluation protocol, metrics, timeline, or participation terms when necessary.

7. Publication and Citation

The organizers may publish the final leaderboard, aggregated results, and analyses of submitted methods. Participants who use the CoSEC dataset or report challenge results in publications are expected to cite the official CoSEC paper or challenge reference provided by the organizers.

Data release

LogiCon will provide enough public data for participants to develop systems, while keeping the test set and labels protected until the submission period has ceased. Data is exposed through Codabench.

Data package checklist

  • Dataset card, license, citation, and known limitations.
  • Stable release tags and checksums.
  • Example submission and schema validator.
  • Policy for external data, pretrained models, tools, and manual annotation.
