Proposed NeurIPS 2026 Competition

LogiCon Competition

Benchmarking logical consistency, constraint following, and reliable reasoning in AI systems.

LogiCon is a competition proposal for evaluating whether models can reason reliably under explicit logical constraints, multi-step dependencies, and adversarially structured inputs. The competition is designed to provide reproducible tasks, transparent metrics, strong baselines, and a public leaderboard.

Scientific motivation

Why LogiCon?

Current AI systems can produce fluent answers while violating explicit constraints, losing consistency across steps, or failing under small perturbations. LogiCon focuses on systematic evaluation of such failures and on methods that improve reliable reasoning rather than surface-level performance.

The competition emphasizes clear task definitions, controlled train/validation/test splits, reproducible baselines, and metrics that reward both correctness and consistency.

Competition goals

  • Define rigorous benchmarks for logical consistency and constraint satisfaction.
  • Encourage methods that are robust, reproducible, and interpretable.
  • Provide accessible baselines for teams with different resource levels.
  • Support post-competition analysis of model behavior and failure modes.

Competition structure

Proposed tracks

The final tracks will be specified in the proposal and competition rules. The structure below supports a low-barrier entry track and a more open research track.

Track A · Prediction

Participants submit predictions for held-out instances. This track is lightweight, easy to reproduce, and suitable for broad participation.

  • CSV/JSONL submissions
  • Public validation leaderboard
  • Hidden final test set

Track B · Systems

Participants submit systems or code that solve tasks under a standardized execution environment.

  • Containerized or notebook-based baseline
  • Resource-normalized evaluation
  • Emphasis on reproducibility

Track C · Analysis

Optional analysis track for diagnostic reports, failure analyses, and interpretability-focused entries.

  • Qualitative and quantitative analysis
  • Scientific insight over leaderboard rank
  • Post-competition synthesis

Data release

Dataset plan

The competition will provide a documented dataset with train, validation, and hidden test splits. The release will include a data statement, schema, examples, and known limitations.

The final data hosting location will be announced here. Candidate hosting options include Hugging Face Datasets, Zenodo, OSF, Codabench, Kaggle, or another stable public archive depending on data size and licensing constraints.

Planned release components

  • Dataset card and license.
  • Schema and example instances.
  • Train/validation splits for development.
  • Hidden test set for final ranking.
  • Baseline outputs and reproducibility notes.

Evaluation protocol

Transparent metrics and reproducible scoring

LogiCon will evaluate performance across correctness, consistency, robustness, and resource-aware reproducibility. Final formulas will be published before launch.

1Task correctness on hidden examples.
2Constraint satisfaction and internal consistency.
3Robustness to controlled perturbations.
4Reproducibility, efficiency, and reporting quality.

Participation

Rules and eligibility

Detailed rules will be finalized before launch. The intended rules emphasize fairness, transparency, data integrity, and responsible use of external resources.

  • Teams may participate remotely.
  • External data and models must be disclosed according to track rules.
  • Final submissions may require reproducibility materials.
  • Participants must respect dataset licenses and privacy constraints.
  • Organizers may audit suspicious submissions before final ranking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is LogiCon officially accepted by NeurIPS?

Not yet. This is a proposal website prepared for the NeurIPS 2026 Competition Track. The site will be updated if the competition is accepted.

Can participants start now?

Not yet. The official dataset, rules, submission instructions, and leaderboard will be released after acceptance and infrastructure finalization.
